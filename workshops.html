The idea

Touch is a language most of us never got taught.

These sessions are a place to learn it — gently, in company. You’ll be guided through simple, safe ways to give massage to a partner and to receive it in return, with no experience needed and no pressure to be an expert. What people leave with is less a technique than a felt sense of how much easier the body settles in good company.
