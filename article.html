Shedding what the body has stored

The Approach · 6 min read

Most of us arrive in a session carrying more than the presenting complaint. Beneath the neck tension or the sleeplessness is often something older — a pattern of bracing, a habit of suppression, an emotion that never fully moved through.

This is not a fixed protocol. It is a responsive practice. Each session begins with a conversation — not to take a history, but to understand what your body is carrying right now. From there, we choose the approach that fits: deep tissue release, Ayurvedic oil therapy, Thai bodywork, or something quieter — breath, attention, stillness.

The body already knows something the mind hasn’t caught up to yet.

Working with the threshold

Gently modifying the threshold of discomfort can reset the brain’s interpretation of what it can handle, and in doing so return access to physicality and mobility that had quietly narrowed. The aim is never to push through pain — it is to widen the window in which you can stay present with sensation.

After the session

You leave with simple practices to carry the work forward: ways to notice what is in the body right now, and ways to calm your own nervous system when things get hard. Over a few sessions, awareness becomes something closer to intuition.
